Banknote images

Reproducing banknotes is restricted by anti-counterfeiting law in many countries, separately from copyright — the ECB, for one, requires reproductions to be at most 75% or at least 125% of actual size, or marked SPECIMEN. This app cannot check whether an export complies: nothing in the data gives a note's physical dimensions.
