Cross-sections

The rise and fall of the land along a line across a country, from the coast to the mountains, with no names.

The line

Heights

Off: every country is drawn on the same scale, from sea level to 6,000 m, so flat countries look flat. On: the heights fill the chart, which shows small hills but makes every country look hilly.

Caption

A short line under the chart, e.g. "The line runs through the capital."

Export

Pick a country to walk across it. The land along a straight line from one border to the other: where the mountains rise, how wide the plains are and where the coast drops away is the clue.
