Shaded relief

A country's mountains and plains as a shaded relief — no borders, no rivers, no names.

Terrain

Heights are exaggerated to suit the country; this makes slopes steeper or gentler.
Every scheme spans the same heights, so low countries stay low-coloured.

Sheet

Blended: the background takes the ground colour at the country's edge, so the outline fades. Change the ground colour, or the height colours, to change it.

Caption

A short explanation for the players, e.g. "Heights are exaggerated."

Export

Pick a country to see its mountains and plains. Lit from the north-west like an old atlas, with no borders, water or names: where the ranges run, how wide the plains are and where the land is flat is the clue.
