As text

Sources

No single body collects baby names worldwide. These lists come from Wikipedia's compilation, which in turn cites national registries, newspapers and, for some countries, polls such as BabyCenter's. Each list says what it is: its year, whether it counts newborns or everyone's names, and whether its source is unofficial.
